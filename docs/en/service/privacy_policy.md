# Privacy Policy

## About privacy on this instance

c30 (ced) (hereafter "we"), who operates and manages the distributed microblogging SNS service "Juice Server" (hereafter "this service"), sets forth this Privacy Policy (hereafter "this Policy") regarding the handling of privacy information on this service, as follows.

## Personal information collected by this service

### Article 1 (Definition of privacy information)

1. Among privacy information, "personal information" refers to "personal information" as defined under the Act on the Protection of Personal Information, meaning information about a living individual that can identify a specific individual through information such as their date of birth, address, phone number, or other descriptions contained in that information.
2. Among privacy information, "history and characteristic information" refers to information other than what is defined as "personal information" above, and includes web pages viewed by the user, search keywords used, date/time and method of use, usage environment, age, the user's IP address, cookie information, location information, device identification information, etc.

### Article 2 (Method of collecting privacy information)

1. This service may ask for personal information such as an email address when a user registers for an account.
2. In addition to the above, this service may also ask for additional personal information such as date of birth to confirm that a user meets the age requirements set forth in the Terms of Service of this service.
3. This service collects history and characteristic information about users (including, when using a mobile device such as a smartphone, the communication status of that device and various setting information related to its use) when a user uses or views this service.

### Article 3 (Purpose of collecting and using personal information)

The purposes for which this service collects and uses personal information are as follows:

1. To display registration information and information related to services used, in order for users to view or modify their own registration information and view their usage status
2. To use contact information such as an email address for the purpose of identity verification of the user
3. To use an email address to notify or contact users regarding this service
4. To transfer information registered with this service to other servers based on instructions from the user, in order to make it easier for the user to input data
5. To use inquiry content, information necessary for providing the service to the user, the user's service usage status, contact information, etc., in order to respond to inquiries from users
6. Purposes incidental to the above purposes of use

### Article 4 (Provision of personal information to third parties)

1. This service will not provide personal information to third parties without obtaining prior consent from the user, except in the following cases.\
   However, this does not apply to cases permitted under the Act on the Protection of Personal Information or other laws, or to a date of birth registered as the user's public information.
   1. When based on laws and regulations
   2. When necessary for the protection of a person's life, body, or property, and it is difficult to obtain the consent of the person
   3. When particularly necessary for improving public health or promoting the sound growth of children, and it is difficult to obtain the consent of the person
   4. When cooperation with a national institution, local government, or a third-party organization entrusted by them is necessary for them to perform duties prescribed by law, and obtaining the consent of the person could interfere with the performance of that duty
   5. When the following have been announced or disclosed in advance:
      1. That the purpose of use includes provision to a third party
      2. The items of data provided to the third party
      3. The means or method of provision to the third party
2. Notwithstanding the above, the following are not considered a "third party":
   1. Operating members of this service, including moderators
   2. Cases where all or part of the handling of personal information is entrusted to the extent necessary to achieve the purpose of use of this service
   3. Cases where personal information is provided in connection with a business succession due to a merger or other reason
   4. Cases where personal information is jointly used with a specific party, and the fact of such joint use, the items of personal information jointly used, the scope of joint users, the purpose of use of the personal information by joint users, and the name of the person responsible for managing that personal information have been notified to the person in advance, or made readily available for the person to know

### Article 5 (Disclosure of personal information)

When a request for disclosure of personal information is made by the person themselves, this service will disclose it to that person without delay.\
However, we may not disclose all or part of it if doing so falls under any of the following. If we decide not to disclose it, we will notify the person of that fact without delay.\
Please note that a fee of 1,500 yen will be charged per request for disclosure of personal information.

1. When there is a risk of harming the life, body, property, or other rights and interests of the person or a third party
2. When there is a risk of significantly interfering with the proper operation of this service
3. When it would otherwise violate laws and regulations
4. Notwithstanding the above, information other than personal information that falls under history and characteristic information will, in principle, not be disclosed.

### Article 6 (Correction or deletion of personal information)

1. If the personal information held by this service about a user is incorrect, the user may request that this service correct the personal information through the prescribed procedures of this service, except in the following cases:
   1. When the account is suspended due to misconduct or other reasons
   2. When we determine it is not appropriate
   3. When it is technically impossible
2. If this service receives a request as described above and determines that it needs to respond to that request, it will correct the personal information in question without delay and notify the user of this.

### Article 7 (Changes to this Policy)

1. The content of this Policy may be changed without notice to users.
2. Except where otherwise specified by this service, the revised Policy shall take effect once it is posted on [this site](https://docs.mk-juice.dev/).
