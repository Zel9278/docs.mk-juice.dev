# Widget position setting

A personal setting that lets you choose whether the desktop's persistent widget panel, or the mobile widget drawer, is displayed on the left or right side of the screen.

## How to change it

From the settings screen at `/settings/juice`, you can choose "Left" or "Right" (default is Right).

- Desktop: switches which side the persistent widget panel is displayed on.
- Mobile: switches the slide-in direction of the widget drawer (opened from the "App" icon in the footer).

This setting controls which side the widget display as a whole appears on — it is not a feature for arranging individual widgets separately on the left and right.
