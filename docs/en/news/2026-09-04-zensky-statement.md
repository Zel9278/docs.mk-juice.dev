# A Statement to ZenSky Project and "Misskey.Tokyo"

September 4, 2026

I am **ced ([@c30](https://mk-juice.dev/@c30))**, the developer of [Misskey Juice (JUICE)](https://github.com/Zel9278/misskey-juice).

Following ZenSky Project's publication of "[Our organization's position and statement regarding the dispute between Misskey.Tokyo and Misskey-juice](https://s-zensky.com/press-1.html)" concerning the "Misskey.Tokyo" instance that ZenSky Project provides and operates, I have organized my **personal views** on the matter into a few points below.

## 1. To begin with

Misskey Juice (JUICE), which I develop, originally implemented a "feature that lets you react to announcements" on [Misskey.art](https://misskey.art) (hereafter "Art") and provided it to Art's users.

This feature was very well received by Art's users, so under the theme of "it would be nice to have original features like this," I carried the "feature that lets you react to announcements" over from Misskey.art and started a new, personal development project under the name Misskey Juice (JUICE).

## 2. The circumstances behind Misskey Juice (JUICE) and Juice Server being forked and opened up

As explained above, Misskey Juice (JUICE) is developed under the main theme of "it would be nice to have original features like this," and I incorporate new ideas on a daily basis.

On August 23, 2026, when [Askey](https://misskey.tokyo/@askey) approached me asking, "Would you be willing to run Misskey.Tokyo?", I agreed to their proposal and moved the conversation forward, based on an idea I had already been considering: "Could I develop, as my own fork, new features on top of what's already implemented in Art?"

However, ZenSky Project's [Press Release No. 1](https://s-zensky.com/press-1.html), published on September 3, states the following (quoted verbatim from the original):

> At first, we received roughly the following proposal from c30, the developer of Misskey-juice: since he was developing Misskey-juice, he wanted it adopted as a semi-official server of Misskey.Tokyo. In exchange, he would ask Misskey.Tokyo what features it wanted.

As of now, Misskey.Tokyo has neither implemented Misskey Juice (JUICE) nor provided it to its users in any way. Furthermore, the statement contains claims entirely different from the original conversation, and content suggesting private use of Misskey Juice (JUICE).

The main factors that led to this situation include:

- A gap in understanding between the two of us (myself and Askey)
- Insufficient confirmation and communication in our subsequent exchanges

As a result, a significant discrepancy arose without **proper dialogue** or **sufficient mutual understanding** being achieved.

Going forward, to avoid situations like this, I will place even greater importance on communication with users and continue to work diligently on the development of Misskey Juice (JUICE).

## 3. To users who wish to use Misskey Juice (JUICE) going forward, centered on Juice Server

Misskey Juice (JUICE) is currently in development, and I operate **the instance I run myself, "Juice Server,"** as the **official instance**, for the purpose of early testing of features I want to implement myself, as well as fixing bugs and debugging.

As part of this instance's operating policy, I enable bugs I haven't anticipated myself, as well as features still under verification, ahead of time; it serves largely a testing purpose. Because of this, I will add and fix various features in future updates before providing users with a **stable version** of **Misskey Juice (JUICE)**.

For details, please see [About this instance's operating policy](../about-juice-server.md).

## 4. In closing

I sincerely apologize to Askey and to our users for the great inconvenience this has caused.

Misskey Juice (JUICE) and Juice Server will continue to aim for better fork development and service delivery, not through my efforts alone, but with everyone's cooperation.

I humbly ask for your continued support of Misskey Juice (JUICE) and Juice Server going forward.
